Questions

Why only Saturday to Tuesday?

Taking orders early in the week lets us grill and ship everything fresh, so it doesn't sit in transit over the weekend.

How spicy is it?

Traditional suya has a peppery kick. Tell us mild, medium or hot when you order and we'll adjust the yaji.

How do I pay?

We'll go over payment options when we confirm your order by phone.

How should I reheat it?

Warm in the oven at 350°F for 5–8 minutes or in an air fryer for 3–4 minutes. Avoid the microwave if you can — it softens the crust.

What is suya?

Suya (also called tsire) is a traditional Hausa smoke-grilled spiced meat on a skewer from Northern Nigeria, coated in yaji — a blend of ground peanuts, ginger, chili and spices.
